# Illuuminati.org (`illuu.org`)

> **Building Nepal's Digital Platform & Core Information Infrastructure.**

Illuuminati.org is designing connected, modular digital systems that organize, verify, and scale Nepal's information, opportunities, businesses, tourism, and technology capabilities.

---

## The 777 Framework

We structure information and services with a consistent geographic framework designed around Nepal’s administrative architecture:

$$\text{Provinces (7)} \longrightarrow \text{Districts (77)} \longrightarrow \text{Local Governments (753)}$$

This ensures our platforms provide localized relevance while maintaining national scale and global interoperability.

## Technical Architecture & Principles

- **Shared Core:** Standardized protocols for identity, location context, and structured data across all platforms.
- **Infrastructure First:** Reusable APIs, intelligence layers, and software systems designed to convert real-world complexity into actionable insight.
- **Open Standards:** Built for scalable interoperability, security, and high reliability across urban and rural connectivity conditions.
